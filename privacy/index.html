Privacy policy

RallyReel collects no data about you. Your videos, scores and stats stay on your iPhone unless you choose to share them.

Last updated: October 4, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. RallyReel has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics, and it doesn't use third-party tracking or data-collection tools. We don't receive your videos, scores, stats, contacts or location.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything RallyReel makes is stored in the app on your iPhone: match recordings, the scores you tap, player names, trims, serve analysis, stats and reels. All analysis, including finding where points start and where serves land, happens on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes this data.

If you turn on iCloud Backup in RallyReel's settings, your recordings are included in your iPhone's own iCloud backup, which Apple stores under Apple's privacy policy. We can't access it.

Apple Watch and the phone remote

Score taps from your Apple Watch go directly to your iPhone. The phone remote works directly between your camera iPhone and the scoring phone, over Bluetooth, a direct Wi-Fi link, your local Wi-Fi or your iPhone's Personal Hotspot. Nothing passes through our servers; we don't have any. When you set the table corners from the remote, a still picture from the camera is sent to the scoring phone only. If you save a hotspot password to show the join code, it's kept in your iPhone's Keychain.

When you share

Reels and clips leave your iPhone only when you share them, save them to Photos, or upload them yourself, for example to YouTube or a messaging app. From then on, the app or service you shared to handles them under its own privacy policy.

If you contact us

If you email support or send feedback, we receive your email address and whatever you include, and use it only to reply and improve RallyReel. Beta feedback sent through TestFlight is handled by Apple and passed to us. Ask us at any time to delete your messages.

Children

RallyReel doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If RallyReel ever starts collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens, with a new date at the top.
