Your table tennis matches, cut down to the points.

Record a match on your iPhone, tap who won each point from your Apple Watch or another phone, and RallyReel trims away the ball-fetching and the walking back. You get a Full match Reel and a Highlights Reel, with the score on screen.

How it works

  1. Set up the camera

    Put your iPhone on a tripod behind your end of the table, high enough to see all four corners of the table.

  2. Score each point

    Tap who won on your Apple Watch, or let a friend score from their phone. No Wi-Fi needed.

  3. Create a Reel

    One tap builds the full match or just your ★ points, ready to share or save to Photos.

What you get

Clips that start at the serve

RallyReel follows the ball to find where each point begins, and flags the few it isn't sure about so you can check them in seconds.

Broadcast-style graphics

Player names at each end, a live scoreboard, and a match summary when it's over.

Highlights across matches

Pick your best points from a week or a whole season and build one Reel.

Performance

Your record against each opponent, your form, and where your serves land (serve maps in beta).

Scoring without Wi-Fi

A second iPhone connects straight to the camera iPhone. Any other phone can score from its web browser.

Ready by morning

Plug your iPhone in overnight and RallyReel finds the point starts and analyzes serves while it charges. Reels are ready in one tap.

Your matches stay on your iPhone

RallyReel has no accounts and no analytics. Your videos, scores and stats never leave your phone unless you share them yourself. Read the privacy policy.

Try it with your club

RallyReel is in beta through TestFlight, Apple's app for trying new apps before they reach the App Store. Open the link on your iPhone, install TestFlight if asked, then tap Install. It's free.

You'll need an iPhone with iOS 17 or later. An Apple Watch is optional: a second phone can do the scoring instead.
